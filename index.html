About me

I'm a 15 yo. Student and Engineering Enthusiast from Slovakia.

I mainly work on 3D modeling parametric models for 3D printing and develop custom software projects using C++. My focus is on combining CAD design with embedded systems, such as programming ESP32 microcontrollers to control physical hardware. I enjoy taking ideas from initial concepts all the way to finished, working on physical or digital products. Alongside hardware and coding, I also keep up with digital trends and modern toolsets to continuously expand my technical skillset and knowledge.
